Song Release — "முப்பாலின் முதல்வர்" (Chief of Muppal)

A song about Thiruvalluvar, "முப்பாலின் முதல்வர்", was released at the 12th Street Award Ceremony organized by the Alternative Media Center at Velammal Higher Secondary School, Chennai.

Credits:

  • Music Composition: V.K. Kannan
  • Singer: Aadi Gopal
  • Lyrics: Naatiyam Neelakanthan
  • Music by: A.R. Rehana
  • Dance: Hema Latha Ramasamy (choreography by Vinod)

Dignitaries Present: Justice R. Mahadevaan (Chennai High Court), V.G. Santhosam (Thiruvalluvar Award recipient, Tamil Nadu government)

Foundation Leadership: President Mala Gopal, Secretary Aadi Gopal, Event Coordinator Meera Srikant, with participants Gayatri and Ramasamy from Houston.

Thirukkural Album — All 133 Chapters Released

The Kural Koodal Semmozhi Foundation has released all 1,330 Thirukkural verses set to music, featuring singer Aadi Gopal, composer V.K. Kannan, and explanations by Kural Soodi Umaiyaal. The album is available on all major streaming platforms.
